YOUR OWN PHOTOGRAPHS
====================

This complete website includes illustrative sample photos so it looks finished immediately.
The samples are not photographs of you or your actual projects.

HOW TO REPLACE THE SIX PERSONAL PHOTO GALLERY IMAGES
1. Select six of your photos.
2. Rename them exactly:
    personal-01.jpg
    personal-02.jpg
    personal-03.jpg
    personal-04.jpg
    personal-05.jpg
    personal-06.jpg
3. Put them in the SAME folder as index.html. Replace the six sample images.
4. Upload the changed files to GitHub and commit.

If photos are PNG, WEBP or HEIC, convert/export to JPG first or update the file
extensions in index.html and script.js.

REPLACE ENGINEERING EXAMPLE IMAGES
Change the image files while keeping their original names:
    sonar-lab.jpg      (your sonar prototype / real workbench)
    robotic-hand.jpg   (your SolidWorks render or hand prototype)
    fpga-lab.jpg       (your real FPGA + AFE PCB)
    gearbox.jpg        (your gearbox experiment or diagram)
    campus.jpg         (your CQU campus photo)
    chongqing.jpg      (your own city photo)
    hero-collage.jpg   (your own hero/header montage)

The visible "Concept illustration" tags can be removed from index.html once
an image has been replaced by an actual photograph of your own work.

For best results: export JPEG around 1400px wide, 75-85% quality, usually <500KB.
Avoid publishing private identifiers, lab secrets or portraits without permission.
